\subsection{Optimization of Marketing Strategy for Coupon Package}[05/2024~--~Present]

\begin{itemize}
  \itemsep 0.3em
  \item Coupon package is an important marketing tool, aiming to boost growth targets by leveraging users' loss aversion psychology. The strategy indirectly lowers the package price through different distribution of discount, promoting the conversion of users and thus driving growth.
  \item In the early stage of store integration\footnotemark[2], operations put manually differentiated offers to user segments based on \textit{Order-AOV} combinations. The algorithm adopted an ``ML + OR" framework for \textbf{uplift modeling} of different treatment actions. The main challenges included: 1) a large action space; 2) significant budget fluctuations; 3) difficulty in modeling the \textit{Actual Payment}.
  \item The v1.0 strategy, limited by RCT data volume, chose to model \textit{Order} as the target. To address large differences between online and offline costs, high-subsidy treatment mappings were designed to expand the cost decision space. Online, three A/B tests were conducted: 1) Lagrangian allocation at the user segment level, 2) Lagrangian allocation at the user level, and 3) a robust, heuristic-based 0-1 treatment strategy. Solution 2 was fully rolled out, increasing CLC orders by +0.96\% and actual payment by +0.42\%.
  \item The v2.0 strategy mainly optimized the \textit{GTV} metric. As the GTV fluctuated greatly, it has a pronounced long-tail effect, and a low SNR. Therefore, the GTV target was decomposed as $\text{GTV} = \text{Order} \times \text{AOV}$, with separate modeling for each. A concave function transformation was applied to the AOV to enhance incremental signals in the main part of the probability density and reduce noise in the extremes, thus improving the SNR. In addition, the two-stage framework was replaced with an end-to-end DFL solution. This combined solution rolled out and increased CLC orders by +0.71\% and actual payment by +0.32\%.
  \item The v2.2 strategy adopted the MMoE model, using multi-objective modeling to differentiate across business units benefiting from MMoE's multi-task learning capabilities. This solution was fully rolled out, increasing CLC orders by +0.35\% and actual payment by +0.26\%.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{STLformer: Forecasting with STL Decomposition and Rank Correlation}[03/2023]

\vspace{-\parskip}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Proposed STLformer, a Transformer-based time series forecasting model.
  \item STLformer progressively decomposes time series with STL decomposition and models seasonal and long-term trend patterns with an attention-based encoder and decoder, respectively.
  \item STLformer tests the ARCH effect and uses Spearman's $\rho$-based attention to model nonlinear dependencies.
  \item STLformer achieves forecasting in $\mathcal{O}(N \log{N})$ and outperforms SOTA methods on multiple datasets, especially on nonlinear dependent series (e.g., financial series).
\end{itemize}
